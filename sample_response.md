## 6️⃣ Final Recommendations  

1. **Book flights early** (at least 4‑5 weeks in advance) to lock the ₹24 k round‑trip price. Use price‑alert tools on Skyscanner, Cleartrip or the airline’s own site.  
2. **Reserve accommodation** (especially the Pangong lakeside camp) **at least 2 weeks** before your travel dates – they fill up quickly in the peak season.  
3. **Hire a local, licensed driver** who knows high‑altitude routes. The cost is already included in the budget and gives you flexibility to stop for photos or short hikes.  
4. **Carry a basic medical kit** (altitude sickness tablets, pain reliever, band‑aids) and stay hydrated (2–3 L water per day).  
5. **Pack smart:** thermal innerwear, wind‑proof jacket, good trekking shoes, sunglasses, sunscreen (SPF 30+), and a reusable water bottle.  
6. **Travel insurance** (covering trip cancellation & medical evacuation) is advisable – a short‑term plan costs ~₹800‑₹1 200.  
7. **Cash:** ATMs are scarce in Nubra & Pangong; keep **₹5 000–₹7 000 in cash** for those remote stretches.  
8. **Connectivity:** Purchase a local prepaid SIM (Airtel/Vi) at Leh airport for reliable 4G coverage.
